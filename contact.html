03 — Questions

The ones we get most.

Do you work outside NCR?

Yes. We are currently on sites in Pune, Jaipur and Udaipur, and we take projects anywhere we can staff a resident team.

Can we keep our own architect?

Sometimes, if the drawings are buildable and the architect is willing to work inside our model. We will say so honestly at the feasibility stage.

What is the minimum project size?

Roughly 40,000 sq ft of built area. Below that, the integrated model does not earn its cost and we will point you to a good contractor instead.

How do you charge?

A fixed design fee plus construction at open-book cost with an agreed margin. Every trade invoice is visible to the client.

What happens after handover?

Five years of care: 48-hour site response, an annual condition survey, and the as-built model handed over in full.
