02 — The model

A building,
assembled in public.

Every client gets the federated model — structure, services and skin — not a marketing render of it.

01 / Federate

Architecture, structure and services are combined into one model and clash-checked before a single drawing is issued.

02 / Price

Quantities come straight from the model, so the estimate and the design never drift apart between stages.

03 / Build

Site reads the same model the client does. Weekly audits compare the pour against the drawing, in public.

03 — History

Twenty-eight years,
in order.

1998

Founded in a site office

Three engineers, one draughtsman and a leased plot in Sector 44. First project: a twelve-unit walk-up.

2004

Structural team in-house

Stop tendering the frame. Bring the engineer inside the studio and the formwork cycle drops from nine days to six.

2011

First million sq ft

Aravalli Heights phase one completes. The practice crosses a million square feet delivered, with zero claims.

2016

Owned plant

Thirty-one machines bought outright — cranes, batching, formwork systems — to take the schedule off other people's calendars.

2021

Bagh Courtyard opens

First hospitality project and first IGBC Gold. The courtyard typology becomes a house specialism.

2026

Sundial handed over

180 homes, 41 months, eleven snags. The model-first process is now standard on every project.

We have never lost a project to design. We have won every one on delivery.
Arjun Rao · Partner, construction
