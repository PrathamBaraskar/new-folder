01 — The practice

We treat construction as a craft, not a transaction.

Dwaar was founded in 1998 by three site engineers who were tired of watching good drawings die on site. We keep design and construction under one roof, so the team that draws the detail is the team that builds it.

That single decision — no handoffs, no blame between consultants — is why our buildings arrive on schedule, on budget, and without the snag list nobody owns.

  • In-house architecture, structure and MEP
  • Owned formwork, plant and site crews
  • Weekly QA audits, published to the client
  • Five-year care programme after handover

A building should feel like it was always meant to stand there — and it should be built by the people who drew it.

Since 1998

Thirty-eight completed landmarks across seven cities. Every one of them designed, priced and delivered by one team.

03 — How we build

Four stages.
One accountable team.

01 — Survey

We start in the soil, not in the software.

Before a single line is drawn, we test the ground, read the light and price the statutory envelope. Feasibility is a number, not an opinion.

Duration
3–5 weeks
Deliverable
Feasibility report + cost band
02 — Design

One model. Structure, services and skin.

Architecture, structure and MEP are coordinated in a single federated model. Every drawing that leaves the office has already been built once — digitally.

Duration
10–16 weeks
Deliverable
GFC set + approvals
03 — Build

Our crews, our plant, our standard.

We do not sub-contract the structure. Formwork, plant and site supervision are ours, which is why a weekly QA audit is a habit rather than a negotiation.

Duration
18–30 months
Deliverable
Weekly client dashboard
04 — Handover

A snag list that somebody owns.

Handover includes full documentation, as-built drawings and a five-year care programme. If it moves, leaks or settles, you call the same team that built it.

Care period
5 years
Response
48 hours on site
